Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours. No mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots). Do the stages in order; every missable risk is flagged in Stage 1.

    Missable Trophies

    Only one trophy can be missed. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story.
