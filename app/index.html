    Platinum Roadmap — all 55 trophies in 7 stages

    Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 1 missable · 6 unconfirmed. The story runs about 60 hours; the platinum takes about 200. Cheats disable trophies, so keep them off until the platinum pops. Every stage shows its difficulty, hours, guidance and trophy count; per-stage earned counters fill in as you check trophies off.

    Stage 1 The story, start to finish

    Difficulty 3/10 · 60 hours · 12 trophies · 0 / 12 earned

    Play the main missions in order from the opening mission Gone Fishing through the final mission. The 12 story trophies unlock as the story reaches them: 2 chapter trophies in Chapter 1, 3 more across Chapters 2 to 4, the gold finale trophy, and 6 mission trophies (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save before every big mission. Two things to do during the story, not after it: meditate at the Chapter 1 balcony spot before the chapter ends (the one confirmed missable trophy, Positive Mental Attitude) and keep cheats off — they disable trophies.

      Stage 2 Exploration & collectibles

      Difficulty 4/10 · 35 hours · 7 trophies · 0 / 7 earned

      Sweep Leonida region by region rather than criss-crossing the map: 18 meditation spots across 10 locations, 100 WAiNK It spots, 100 vehicles to drive, all 52 regions to visit, 3 full collectible card decks, 10 Scooter Bros deliveries, and 8 purchasable garages. Start the meditation sweep in Chapter 1 (see stage 1) and pick up spots as the story opens new regions. Note the one count clash in the sources: the trophy text says 8 garages, while PowerPyx's property database lists 12 — buy 8 and the trophy should pop; the extra 4 are spares, not requirements.

        Stage 3 Combat & crime

        Difficulty 3/10 · 12 hours · 7 trophies · 0 / 7 earned

        The combat set is short and mostly free-roam: 5 kills with Combat Focus, an escape from the VCIA airport, a Rhino tank takedown, a clean jewelry-store robbery, 5 bank vaults cracked, one armored-truck blitz, and 9 gang operations cleared. Do the bank vaults and gang operations as they appear on the map between other stages — none of them is gated — and save the tank and airport escapes for a quiet moment with full health and a fast getaway car.

          Stage 4 Minigames, races & challenges

          Difficulty 6/10 · 45 hours · 11 trophies · 0 / 11 earned

          The hardest and longest stage — the platinum's real grind. Three big counts dominate it: 250 stunts out of 397 across Leonida, 42 shooting-range golds across 12 ranges, and 50 challenges out of 80. Around those: 14 Top Fun drone races, 20 race wins, 9 different activities for Master of None, the fishing challenge against Cal, the arcade high score, a bowling night, a personal best with your partner, and 500 tracked choices. Chip at stunts and challenges during every other stage instead of leaving all 250 stunts to the end; the shooting-range golds are the only genuinely skill-gated ask, so practice the ranges early and come back for gold once your aim is warm.

            Stage 5 Side strands & misc

            Difficulty 3/10 · 20 hours · 11 trophies · 0 / 11 earned

            Finish the five side-character strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone — plus all 8 stranger missions and the hunt for Bret. These are chapter-gated, not missable, but finish each strand before advancing the story past its window. The misc half is money and partner time: 3 shopping trips with your partner, $100,000 spent in shops, and $250,000 in marked bills moved. Money comes naturally from the story and the stage-3 robberies; if you are short at the end, the repeatable robberies top it up.

              Stage 6 Cleanup & 100%

              Difficulty 5/10 · 28 hours · 6 trophies · 0 / 6 earned

              The 100% completion checklist (A Long Road, gold) opens after the story — work through whatever it still lists, then check the tracker for anything left. The five mystery trophies live here because no source confirms their triggers: PowerPyx asserts methods for In The Beginning…, Capital Offense and The Truth Is Out There, which four other guides leave unconfirmed, while Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin are unknown everywhere. Try the asserted methods, but do not grind blind — these five are the likeliest to get confirmed methods from the community after launch, so check the cited sources again before sinking hours into them.

                Stage 7 Platinum

                Difficulty 1/10 · 0 hours — pops automatically · 1 trophy · 0 / 1 earned

                Vice Got a Price! pops automatically with the 54th trophy — there is nothing extra to do. If it does not pop, one trophy is still unearned: check the tracker, the 100% checklist, and the five mystery trophies in stage 6 first.
